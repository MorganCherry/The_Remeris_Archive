## Character Creation

Start with a want and a wound. What does your character want more than anything — and what past event makes getting it hard? That tension alone can carry a whole book.

**Useful links:**
- [Reedsy: Character Development Guide](https://blog.reedsy.com/character-development/)
- [NaNoWriMo: Character Building Worksheets](https://nanowrimo.org/)

---

## World Building

Don't dump the whole world in chapter one. Reveal it the way a traveler would discover it — piece by piece, through what the character notices and needs to know right now.

**Useful links:**
- [World Anvil (worldbuilding tool)](https://www.worldanvil.com/)
- [Brandon Sanderson's Laws of Magic (lecture series on YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=brandon+sanderson+laws+of+magic)

---

## Plot Structure

Three acts is a starting point, not a cage: setup, escalating conflict, resolution. If you're stuck, ask "what's the worst thing that could happen right now?" — then consider doing it.

**Useful links:**
- [Save the Cat! Beat Sheet](https://savethecat.com/beat-sheet)

---

## Staying Motivated

Word count goals work better than time goals for most people — "500 words" is a finish line, "one hour" just feels like a chore. Track daily and let momentum do the rest.
